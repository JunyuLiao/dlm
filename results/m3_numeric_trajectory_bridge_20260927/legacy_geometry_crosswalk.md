**Thin-adapter verdict.** Reusing the selector *mathematics and indexed bitmap* is feasible as a bounded reference. Reusing its execution directly is not a thin import: the old path owns vLLM `TritonAttentionImpl` metadata, paged cache/page-table addressing, full CUDA-graph static buffers, operation/guard pointers and `[Hkv,Qgroup,KV32]` maps. The current native path takes Q/K/V through its own router and Q16/KV64 consumer. An adapter would have to specify the query-head/position inverse map, native mask and partial-tile legality, KV32 bitmap address and page/layout translation, preallocated statistics, phase control, and output strides, then time all gather/copy/control costs. Merely replicating Q128 support into smaller programs reproduces its mask and cannot expose finer selection. GLOBAL GQA heads already share KV storage; grouping eight heads does not imply an 8× traffic reduction. The two decision partitions are not nested, so neither is universally sparser.

Haowei's pinned `chw/value_aware@b23f969a3c52417ba84a999ff7a31e3dd00bb697` provides a *criterion/interface clue*, not a plug-compatible native consumer. Its [`value_aware_offline.py`](https://github.com/coconight01/dlm_test/blob/b23f969a3c52417ba84a999ff7a31e3dd00bb697/value_aware_offline.py) describes per-native-KV-head, per-KV-block V RMS/reference statistics and a per-query-head streaming skip rule; [`apply_value_aware_patch.py`](https://github.com/coconight01/dlm_test/blob/b23f969a3c52417ba84a999ff7a31e3dd00bb697/apply_value_aware_patch.py) injects a per-block RHS into its fmha-v2 softmax skip test through a borrowed pointer. Its TensorRT-LLM/fmha-v2 ABI and criterion are not the old vLLM map ABI or the current M1/M3 history-state contract. No speed or quality transfer follows from these sources alone.
