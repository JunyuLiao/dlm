# Interim qualification evidence — 2026-10-19 (Hong Kong)

This immutable publication covers six completed instrumentation-qualification
engines, 20 measured requests, and their frozen source/protocol identity.
The parent campaign is still in progress; this is not a completed benchmark.

`receipt.json` verifies original engine markers and their artifact hashes and
reports actual fixed-budget units, LOCAL skips, CUDA captures, and bookend drift.
`qualification_aggregates.json` contains audit-only exploratory aggregates. Its
accuracy and instrumented times are not clean quality or latency comparisons.

The K4096/K2048 bookends differ in outputs/calls on one of two questions each,
with unchanged scores. These checks do not resolve native clean repeatability.
The completed HumanEval164 results remain separately published under
`results/humaneval_value_k256_20261018/attempt001/analysis/attempt001/`; their
audit-versus-clean divergence remains explicitly unresolved.

Live screens, raw generations, prompts, token IDs, and private execution caches
are excluded. Existing paused studies remain paused.
