# Publication boundary

The complete accepted panel is attempt003. Its public numerical receipts
redact integer native stop-token IDs and exclude private inputs, token IDs,
generations, tensors and logs. Its exact protocol and source hashes remain
unchanged.

The earlier attempt001 qualification execution and attempt002 pilot run
receipts remain intact locally and are ignored: some contain raw native
stop-token metadata. Their sanitized qualification summary, resource counts,
launch metadata and source fingerprints can be published separately. An
ignored historical receipt is not a new public result or part of the accepted
450-cell panel. No historical receipt was rewritten to achieve redaction.
