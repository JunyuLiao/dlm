## Hypotheses and estimands

H1: At equal physical work and matched present bias/mass, a predeclared signed
response or decision score predicts the effect of sparse support on future native
state and first-canvas stopping better than current error and matched sham.
H2: Reusing a support changes that effect beyond a one-call pulse, with either
beneficial persistence or harmful interaction. H2 is distinct from merely measuring
two consecutive applications of sparsity.

Primary mechanism contrast is SA-AA at the second eligible call and remaining
first-canvas native calls. AS-AA separates fresh direct pruning; SS-SA estimates
adding a second sparse application on the sparse-induced state. SS-SA-AS+AA is a
scalar interaction contrast. These contrasts share a restored initial state/RNG.
They do not change the native sampler. Match RNG at restoration and preserve draw
order through aligned calls; final RNG hashes need not agree when legitimate
native stopping produces different total call counts. Any exact candidate evaluation is charged
as explicit diagnostic model work, never presented as a cheap deployed selector.
